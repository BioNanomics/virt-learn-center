# Nursing anatomy resource map

Working collection, September 11, 2026. Scope assumption: entry-level, prelicensure BSN education at IU Fort Wayne, following the project context. This is a starter source map across all 11 major body systems, with proposed teaching objectives. It is not a complete model inventory or a faculty-approved curriculum.

**A nursing-focused collection is feasible.** Combine a broad anatomical atlas for orientation with selected detailed organ models, then attach course objectives, guided views, explanations, and assessments. Hannah Newey’s heart remains the visual benchmark. Comparable quality, complete anatomical coverage, and reusable downloads have not been established for every system.

Here, “source” means a model or teaching resource. Downloadable artwork, editable authoring files, viewer source code, and a hosted interactive presentation are separate deliverables; a link to one does not establish availability of the others.

## Selected heart reference

[Hannah Newey — Cardiac Anatomy: External view of human heart](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cardiac-anatomy-external-view-of-human-heart-a3f0ea2030214a6bbaa97e7357eebd58)

- **Provenance:** the author describes creating it in ZBrush for an MSc Medical Art project at the University of Dundee, with anatomical accuracy confirmed by anatomy lecturer Luke Reid. This is the author's account, not an independent review by IU.
- **Presentation:** external heart anatomy with annotations. The project’s [visual quality and interaction standard](anatomy-visual-quality-standard.md) records the earlier visual inspection.
- **Listing checked:** the browser page displayed a download option, approximately 3 million triangles, and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 on September 11, 2026. The downloadable files and their formats were not inspected.
- **Proposed nursing use:** identify external landmarks and relate coronary vessels to myocardial perfusion in a faculty-authored case.
- **Additional material needed:** internal chambers and valves, conduction anatomy, circulation pathways, and whole-thorax relationships. An external surface model does not supply these lessons by itself.

Dundee’s [Cardiac Anatomy collection](https://sketchfab.com/anatomy_dundee/collections/cardiac-anatomy-e95b3ba669144e57badb640fbcf83bd9) also lists coronary-artery and cardiac-vein models. They are candidates for the next review, with their individual terms and anatomy still to be checked.

## What “nursing requirements” should mean

Use three layers of alignment:

1. **Local course objectives determine required anatomy and depth.** IU Fort Wayne’s accessible 2024–2025 BSN bulletin lists BIOL 20300/20400 anatomy and physiology, NURS-B244/B245 assessment, NURS-B260 fundamentals, and NURS-B261 pathophysiology/pharmacology. These establish a useful course context, but do not establish the current cohort’s requirements. Obtain the applicable syllabi, lab identification lists, and assessment rubrics before claiming complete alignment. [IU Fort Wayne bulletin](https://www.bulletins.iu.edu/iuftw/2024-2025/schools/nursing/undergraduate/BSN/bsn_schoolrequire.shtml)
2. **AACN supplies competency context.** Domain 1, entry-level 1.2a supports applying scientific knowledge; 1.3a concerns clinical reasoning. A lesson can contribute evidence toward these through an explanation and application task. Structure identification alone does not demonstrate the full competency. Proposed mappings require faculty review. [AACN Domain 1 and progression indicators](https://www.aacnnursing.org/essentials/tool-kit/domains-concepts/knowledge-for-nursing-practice)
3. **NCLEX supplies an additional practice-oriented check.** The 2026 RN test plan is effective April 1, 2026–March 31, 2029 and includes Client Needs categories, nursing activities, and clinical judgment. Use it to check the relevance of case applications, not as an exhaustive anatomy checklist. [NCSBN test plans](https://www.nclex.com/test-plans.page)

[OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e) and its [index](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/index) provide a common reference for terminology and foundational explanations. The chapter references below are a proposed organizing aid, not an assertion that IU has adopted this textbook.

Add a shared foundation on anatomical position, patient left/right, planes, cavities, cells, and tissues (OpenStax chapters 1–4). Include regional and cross-sectional views where they improve assessment teaching. Pediatric, pregnancy, aging, anatomical variation, and pathological examples need explicit entries; one adult reference body cannot represent all of them.

## Turning sources into nursing lessons

For every selected resource, keep one record containing:

- Stable resource ID, exact model URL, author, version/date, and review status.
- Visible structures, missing structures, terminology, saved views, and annotation mapping.
- Local course/objective ID, learner level, proposed AACN mapping, and nursing application.
- Prompt, expected explanation, faculty rubric, and learning evidence to retain.
- License link and attribution; download availability, file formats, and whether editable source is supplied.
- Visual review against the heart benchmark, device checks, and an accessible 2D/text alternative.

Use the sequence **identify → explain function → apply to a nursing case**. For the heart, that could mean locating an annotated coronary vessel, explaining its role, and reasoning through a faculty-authored perfusion case. Score the learner’s explanation and case response separately from clicks and camera movements.

Anatomy assets need accompanying physiology material. The existing [XR anatomy and physiology review](open-source-xr-anatomy-physiology-review.md) identifies Bodylight and Pulse as candidates when lessons require dynamic behavior. A static organ or an animation alone does not establish a physiological simulation.

## Recommended first expansion

Complete a heart–lungs–kidneys teaching set first, using the existing heart as the visual reference. For each, select an overview, an internal or functional-detail view, and a nursing application. Resolve the required structures with faculty, record asset terms, and compare desktop presentation before headset trials. Then apply the same record and review process to the remaining systems.

The practical completion criterion is **coverage of the approved course objectives**, with visible gaps and reviewed alternatives. This document establishes sources and proposed scope; individual asset selection, full visual review, current syllabus mapping, and device validation remain to be done.
